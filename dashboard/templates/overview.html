Overview

An AI agent that pays for data, and a guard that keeps it honest

The agent buys data from web shops with real USDC on a local copy of Base Sepolia. AI can be tricked by a dishonest shop. The guard can't, because it checks every bill against the owner's rules before any signature exists.

1

The agent asks

A task such as "price and mood for Ether". The agent picks what to buy.

2

The seller bills

The shop answers HTTP 402 with a bill: amount, wallet, token, time limit.

3

The guard checks

Eight plain rules. One failure and the bill is refused, nothing is signed.

4

Money moves

Only allowed bills are signed and settled on-chain. The shop gets paid.

Demo in five steps

Open each view in its own window, side by side.
1Buy something honestly. On the Service provider page, pick Weather, choose Tokyo, press "See the bill", then "Pay and get the data". An invoice appears; the Guard page shows the bill allowed.
2Ask the agent. On the Guard page, ask "What's the weather in Tokyo and the air quality in Delhi?" and watch every step: catalog search, bills, the guard's checks, the block each payment lands in.
3Play Rob the Robot, round 1. On the Attacker page, set the round to "Guard off", pick The Overcharge, launch. The AI pays you 95 cents, on-chain.
4Round 2. Switch to "Guard on" and launch again. Caught: "Price is 95x the usual 1¢". Tune the knobs: the recon panel shows which rules would still catch you.
5Run every heist. The intel board fills in: with the guard on, nothing works; with it off, most heists pay out. Honest purchases go through either way.
